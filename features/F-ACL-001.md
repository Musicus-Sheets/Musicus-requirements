# F-ACL-001 Access by instrument through groups

## Statement

Members of a group get access to all parts tagged with an instrument granted to the group, e.g. group 'Trumpets' reads every trumpet part.

## Rationale

This is the central idea of the brief: access follows metadata, not individual files.
