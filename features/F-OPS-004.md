# F-OPS-004 Encrypted connections

## Statement

All traffic between devices and server can be encrypted (HTTPS), with little effort for the operator.

## Rationale

Passwords and sessions travel over the network; browsers also require HTTPS for secure cookies and the screen wake lock.
