# F-ACC-002 Stay logged in, with control over devices

## Statement

Members stay logged in on their devices for weeks and can see and end their sessions on other devices.

## Rationale

Logging in on stage is not acceptable (G-004), but a lost tablet must be logged out remotely (G-006).
