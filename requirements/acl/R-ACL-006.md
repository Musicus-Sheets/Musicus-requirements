# R-ACL-006 Grants per programme and until a date

## Statement

The system shall allow granting a group all pieces of a programme, and shall allow any grant to expire at a given date.

## Acceptance criteria

- A substitute group granted the Christmas programme until 2026-12-27 loses access on 2026-12-28.
