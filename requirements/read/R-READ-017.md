# R-READ-017 Split wide systems at a barline

## Statement

When a system is so wide that it cannot reach the minimum staff height, the system view should show it in two halves, divided at a barline near its middle, one below the other.

## Acceptance criteria

- On an 8" tablet in landscape, an A4 part with 5.5 mm staves reaches 7 mm staves.

## Notes

Proposal for later (user decision November 2026). Needs barline detection. The prototype showed that the screen width limits the staff to about 0.9–1.0 × its printed size on an 8" tablet (`docs/agents/background.md` §6.1).
