# R-I18N-007 Parts found by components and every key spelling

## Statement

The library search shall find a part by its custom name, its instrument names, its voices and its key in every spelling of the shipped languages (code, glyph and local names such as "Bb", "B♭", German "B"; "Eb", "E♭", "Es"), and shall ignore the connecting words of part names of the shipped languages (English "in") unless the query consists only of such words.

## Rationale

Without a composed name on the server, the index must contain what any reader may type: the reader's own words for keys and the ensemble's instrument names.

## Acceptance criteria

- "Clarinet in B♭ 2", "clarinet bb 2" and "Clarinet B 2" find the part Clarinet, key Bb, voice 2.
- With German shipped ("Es" a German key name), "Horn Es" finds a horn part in E♭ (unit-tested with a second language's table; German is not shipped yet).
- A query "in" alone still finds titles starting with "In".
