# G-007 Offline use and more formats in the future

## Statement

The system can later be extended by an app that works offline with encrypted local storage, and by sheet formats other than PDF.

## Rationale

Concert venues often have no reliable network, and formats such as MusicXML allow features like transposition. The first version must not block these extensions.
