# G-005 Simple and cheap to run

## Statement

A volunteer-run ensemble can install, operate and back up the system on small, inexpensive hardware without special knowledge.

## Rationale

Most ensembles have no IT department and no budget for servers; a Raspberry Pi or a small rented server must be enough.
