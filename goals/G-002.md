# G-002 The right parts reach the right musicians

## Statement

Every musician gets exactly the parts they play, without the librarian having to assign each file to each person.

## Rationale

An orchestra has hundreds of parts and dozens of musicians; assigning files one by one does not scale and leads to mistakes. Musicians must also not see parts that are not meant for them.
