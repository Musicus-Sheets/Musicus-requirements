# G-004 Reliable paperless playing

## Statement

Musicians can read and turn their parts on a tablet during rehearsals and concerts as reliably as on paper, even when the whole ensemble opens the same piece at the same moment.

## Rationale

On stage there is no time for loading screens, logins or failed downloads. When the conductor announces the next piece, everyone opens it at once.
