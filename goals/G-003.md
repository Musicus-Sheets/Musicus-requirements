# G-003 Personal markings

## Statement

Every musician can add their own markings to their parts, keep them, and use different markings for different concerts, without changing the original sheet.

## Rationale

Musicians write breath marks, fingerings, cuts and reminders into their parts. These markings are personal and change from concert to concert.
